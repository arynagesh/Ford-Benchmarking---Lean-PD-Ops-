What the pattern says

Two diagonal blocks, not a full grid. Disruptors and digital-natives (Tesla, BYD, NIO) concentrate in Cluster B — SDV, OTA, speed, feature-driven definition. Legacy platform leaders (Toyota, VW, Hyundai) concentrate in Cluster A — platform reuse, modularity, part carryover — and go blank across Cluster B. BMW is the visible mid-transformation case, building Cluster-B strength off a legacy base.

Ford's move is horizontal. As a legacy OEM, Ford's credible strengths sit in Cluster A; the gap-closing thesis is about acquiring Cluster-B capability (single source of truth, feature-first definition) that the disruptors already hold. The heat-map makes that direction of travel literal.

Read the confidence dots, not just the colors. Legacy Cluster-A ratings lean 🟡 (mgmt/analyst estimates: TNGA, MQB). Disruptor Cluster-B ratings lean 🟢 (teardown + independent analyst indices). The evidence base is uneven by design — corroborate 🟡 cells before they anchor a recommendation.
