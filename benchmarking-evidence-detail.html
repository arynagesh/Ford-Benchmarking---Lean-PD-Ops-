No evidence cards for Ford. This is deliberate.

Why there's nothing to click through to here

Every other company on this page is scored from external, publicly available evidence. Ford is the subject of the benchmarking exercise, not a comparator — its row will be populated by the Step-5 internal baseline, run against this same locked rubric, not by web research. The heat-map's "exp: LAG" hints are the rubric's stated expectations, not findings, and none of them will convert to a rated cell until that baseline exists. Publishing placeholder Ford evidence here would blur exactly the line this project is built to protect — no internal figure gets quoted before it's actually measured.

Methodology note: every point above was verified via web search while building this page (not carried forward from memory). Two attribution errors were caught and corrected in the process — both flagged inline rather than silently fixed, since a COO-facing document should show its own error-correction, not just its conclusions. Points repeating the same underlying source across multiple cells for one company are labeled, so repetition isn't mistaken for independent corroboration.
